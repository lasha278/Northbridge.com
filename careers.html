Careers  /  The interview experience

The interview experience.

Three stages. Transparent by design. We built this process so you can focus on showing us how you think — not on decoding what we want.

3 stages Fully remote possible Decision within 2 weeks Feedback always provided
The process
What to expect, step by step.

Each stage is purposeful. We removed everything that doesn't help us understand how you work.

Step 01
Application & micro screen
~15 min total

Your first touchpoint. We ask for the essentials plus two short assessments that take minutes, not hours. No cover letter required.

CV & contact informationStandard application. Optionally attach a portfolio — work samples, a project, a GitHub link. Not required but welcomed.
90-second situational judgement caseOne realistic scenario from your first week on the job. Pick the most and least effective response. No preparation needed.
5-minute work-style & personality assessmentNot a pass/fail screen. We use this to understand how you work and to place you in a role where you'll thrive.
Step 02
Job-specific skills test
20–30 min

A focused async test tailored to the role you applied for. We test what actually matters day-to-day — not abstract puzzles.

Data interpretationRead a dataset or chart and answer questions about it. We care about your reasoning, not just the final answer.
SQL or Excel logicA short practical question — a query to write, a formula to construct, or a pivot to build. Reflects real work at NorthBridge.
One algorithmic questionStraightforward logic and efficiency. Not competitive programming — we want to see structured thinking under mild time pressure.
Outlier & anomaly detectionA dataset with a planted issue. Identify it, explain it, and describe how you'd handle it in a production environment.
Step 03
Human interviews
60–90 min

Two conversations with people you'd actually work with — a technical deep-dive and a broader conversation about fit and ambitions.

Technical interview with hiring managerWalk us through your test results. Defend your choices. We may extend a problem or ask follow-ups. A dialogue, not an oral exam.
Team & values conversation30 minutes with a senior analyst. We talk about how you work, what you care about, and whether NorthBridge is the right fit right now.
What we look for
Three things that matter most.

Structured thinking

Take a messy problem and break it into something tractable. This matters more than knowing the right answer.

Data fluency

Numbers should tell you a story. We look for people who notice what's missing — not just what's there.

Clear communication

Smart insights are worthless if no one acts on them. Complexity must land simply.
